1. Ceasar
Encrypt
curl --location 'http://127.0.0.1:5000/api/caesar/encrypt' \
--header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
--data '{
    "plain_text": "HUTECH",
    "key": "3"
}'
Decrypt
curl --location 'http://127.0.0.1:5000/api/caesar/decrypt' \
--header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
--data '{
    "cipher_text" : "KXWHFK",
    "key" : "3"
}'
